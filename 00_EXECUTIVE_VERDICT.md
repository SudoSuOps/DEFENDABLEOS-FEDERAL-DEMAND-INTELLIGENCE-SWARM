## 8. KEY LIMITATIONS

The Tribunal identifies the following unproven claims, data gaps, and risks that must not be misrepresented:

### Limitation A: No Direct DefendableOS Product Has Been Procured by the Federal Government

**Status:** UNVERIFIED
- There is NO evidence that any DefendableOS product, service, or subsidiary has been purchased by any federal agency.
- All demand signals are **market-level**, not **customer-level**.
- **Tribunal ruling:** DefendableOS has **zero federal past performance**. This is a barrier to prime contracting that must be acknowledged and mitigated through subcontracting.

### Limitation B: SAM.gov Research Was Snapshot-Based

**Status:** DOCUMENTED LIMITATION
- All SAM.gov data reflects a single-point-in-time snapshot (May 24, 2026).
- Active status may have changed for all 4 active opportunities before this report was read.
- Award amounts, contractor details, and deadlines are as reported at time of research.
- **Tribunal ruling:** All deadlines must be re-verified before action. The NRC opportunity (due May 26) is likely already expired.

### Limitation C: Award Values Are Ceiling Estimates, Not Obligated Spend

**Status:** DOCUMENTED LIMITATION
- The $12.5B figure represents combined ceiling/potential values, not actual obligated dollars.
- OTAs (including the DoD CDAO $800M awards) are exempt from standard USASpending.gov disclosure.
- Actual spending is typically 20-60% of ceiling values.
- **Tribunal ruling:** Report the $12.5B figure as "identified award ceiling value," not "proven revenue opportunity."

### Limitation D: Active Solicitation Pipeline Is Thin

**Status:** VERIFIED CONCERN
