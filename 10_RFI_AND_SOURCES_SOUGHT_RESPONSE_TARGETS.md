## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Each entry follows this structure:
- **Official Record:** SAM.gov notice ID, title, agency, source URL
- **Agency Need:** What the agency is asking for, in plain language
- **Deadline:** Actual date and whether expired or active
- **Requested Information:** What the RFI/SS asks respondents to provide
- **Defendable Fit Assessment:** Honest scoring of DefendableOS alignment (1-10) with rationale
- **Credible Response Requirements:** What must be in a response to be taken seriously
- **Must Build/Document First:** Prerequisites before responding
- **Response Recommended:** YES / NO / CONDITIONAL with explanation

> **DISCLAIMER:** All deadlines shown are as recorded in SAM.gov. Many have expired. Expired RFIs are still valuable — they signal agency demand, reveal evaluation criteria, and often precede formal solicitations. Monitor for recompetes and follow-ons.
