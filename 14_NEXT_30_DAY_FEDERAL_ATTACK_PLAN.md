## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This plan converts the Tribunal's **HONEY + RESPOND FIRST + PARTNER FIRST** verdict into a concrete, week-by-week operational roadmap. Every task has an owner, a deliverable, success criteria, and identified risks/blockers. The plan assumes DefendableOS has **zero federal past performance**, **no SAM.gov registration**, and **no existing federal certifications** -- the most common starting position for AI/cyber startups entering the federal market.

**The 30-day objective:** Get DefendableOS federally registrable, agency-visible, and demonstrably capable of responding to active solicitations within one calendar month. This is not about winning a contract in 30 days -- it is about becoming **eligible and credible** to compete.

**Critical Context:** As of May 24, 2026, only **4 active opportunities** are open on SAM.gov with deadlines May 26-29. The real demand flows through multi-year IDIQs, BPAs, and OTAs that do NOT post individual opportunities. This plan positions DefendableOS to access THAT flow, not just hunt for standalone notices.

### TASK 1.5: Calculate and Document Size Standard Eligibility

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Specific Action** | Calculate 5-year average annual receipts for each NAICS code per SBA regulations (13 CFR 121.104). Verify the company is below the size standard for each target NAICS. |
| **Owner** | Founder / CFO or Accountant |
| **Deliverable** | Size standard calculation worksheet with source documentation |
| **Success Criteria** | 5-year average receipts calculated for NAICS 541519, 541511, 541512, 518210. All values below respective thresholds ($34M, $34M, $34M, $40M). Documentation stored for SBA audit. |
| **Risk/Blocker** | If 5-year average exceeds $34M, the company is NOT a small business for these NAICS and cannot compete for small business set-asides (which represent $69.6B in FY2024). If close to threshold, monitor growth carefully. |
| **Budget** | Free (internal calculation) |
| **Source** | 13 CFR 121.104 (effective 2024: changed from 3-year to 5-year average) |

**Calculation Method:**
```
5-Year Average Annual Receipts = (Year-1 + Year-2 + Year-3 + Year-4 + Year-5) / 5
```
- Use the 5 most recently completed fiscal years
- Include ALL revenue (not just federal)
- Exclude passive income (investments, real estate)
- Affiliate company revenues MUST be included if affiliation rules apply

| **Owner** | BD Lead |

| **Success Criteria** | Opportunity intake takes <30 minutes daily; screening criteria documented; decision matrix (pursue/monitor/pass) applied consistently; no opportunity sits unreviewed for >24 hours. |
| **Risk/Blocker** | Without a structured workflow, opportunities are missed or reviewed inconsistently. The founder must commit to daily review time -- federal deadlines do not wait. |

**Daily Opportunity Intake SOP:**

```
TIME: 8:00 AM EST (federal postings typically drop overnight)
DURATION: 15-30 minutes

STEP 1: Review SAM.gov email alerts (5 lanes)
STEP 2: For each new notice, record in Opportunity Registry (see Task 2.3)
STEP 3: Apply screening criteria:
  - Is the deadline >72 hours away? (If no, flag URGENT)
  - Is NAICS in our registered set? (If no, skip unless registration pending)
  - Is set-aside type we qualify for? (If sole source, verify cert needed)
  - Fit score >5/10? (If no, skip)
  - Do we have the required registrations/certifications? (If no, flag BLOCKED)
STEP 4: Route to action queue:
  - FIT >=8/10 + ELIGIBLE: Immediate action queue
  - FIT 5-7/10 + ELIGIBLE: Monitor queue (weekly review)
  - FIT <5/10 OR INELIGIBLE: Pass (archive for reference)
STEP 5: Update lane tracker with any new agency names, contact info, or recompete signals
```
