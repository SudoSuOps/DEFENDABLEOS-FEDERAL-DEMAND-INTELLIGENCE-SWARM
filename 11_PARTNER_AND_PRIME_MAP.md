**Key Insight:** The federal AI contracting landscape is dominated by large primes (Booz Allen, Palantir, Lockheed Martin) who won massive IDIQ/task-order vehicles in 2024-2025. Most of these primes lack purpose-built AI TEVV documentation platforms — they deliver TEVV as labor services, not software products. This creates a **software-vendor-not-competitor** niche for DefendableOS as a subcontractor that provides the platform layer beneath their labor-driven services.
